# Getting Support with UniDeploy

We want your experience with UniDeploy to be smooth and productive. Here are the best ways to get help:

---

## 📚 Documentation & Guides

- **Platform Overview**: Read [docs/PLATFORM_OVERVIEW.md](docs/PLATFORM_OVERVIEW.md) for full system architecture.
- **Master Product Spec**: Read [product.md](product.md) for platform capabilities, use cases, and workflows.
- **Getting Started Guide**: Visit [unideploy.in/getting-started](https://www.unideploy.in/getting-started) for interactive onboarding.
- **MCP Integration Guide**: Read [docs/UNIDEPLOY_MCP_SETUP.md](docs/UNIDEPLOY_MCP_SETUP.md) for Cursor, Claude Code, and Windsurf setups.

---

## 🐛 Bug Reports & Feature Requests

- **Bug Reports**: Open an issue using our [Bug Report Template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.md).
- **Feature Suggestions**: Propose new sandbox templates or capabilities using our [Feature Request Template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/feature_request.md).

---

## 💳 Billing & Plan Queries

For inquiries regarding Dodo Payments, UPI transactions, GST invoices, or Enterprise compute pools:
- **Email**: [support@unideploy.in](mailto:support@unideploy.in)
- **Website**: Manage subscriptions at [unideploy.in/pricing](https://www.unideploy.in/pricing)
